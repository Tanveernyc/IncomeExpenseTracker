Delete your Income Expense Tracker App account

Income Expense Tracker App is published by True Organic Hub LLC. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time, with or without the app installed.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Tap the profile button at the top right of any tab to open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm as well.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@trueorganichub.com with the subject Delete my account, from the email address you use to sign in. If you signed in with Apple's Hide My Email, send it from the Apple ID that owns the account and tell us so. We will reply to that address to confirm the request, then delete the account and tell you when it is done, within 30 days and usually much sooner.

What is deleted

Your sign-in and every record in the account: properties and households, expenses, income, recurring rules, and custom categories. If you signed in with Apple, we also cancel the app's Sign in with Apple link.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on purpose. The records are removed from the live database immediately. Our database provider's encrypted backups roll over within its retention window, after which no copy remains. Export your data first (Settings → Export data) if you want to keep a copy, because deletion cannot be undone.

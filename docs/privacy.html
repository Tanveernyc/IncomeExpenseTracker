Income Expense Tracker App Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Income Expense Tracker App is an income and expense tracker for property owners and households, published by True Organic Hub LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address. If you sign up with an email and password, the password is stored only as a one-way hash. If you sign in with Google, Google also shares your name and profile picture address with our sign-in provider; the app does not display or use them. If you sign in with Apple, we ask Apple for your email address only, and you can choose Apple's "Hide My Email" relay address.
  • What you enter: ledgers (names, optional addresses, purchase dates and prices, notes), income and expense entries (amounts, dates, categories, vendors or sources, notes), your own categories, and recurring entries you set up.
  • An account identifier that links your records to you.

We do not collect your location, contacts, bank or card details, device identifiers for advertising, or usage analytics. The app never connects to your bank; every amount is one you typed.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: to sign you in, store your records, show them back to you, and export them when you ask. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, share it with data brokers, or track you across other apps and websites. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

Records are stored with our infrastructure provider, Supabase, in a PostgreSQL database on servers in the United States. Supabase processes the data on our behalf and may not use it for its own purposes. Data travels over encrypted connections (TLS) and is encrypted at rest by the provider.

Database rules allow each account to read and change only its own records. On your iPhone, your sign-in session is kept in the iOS Keychain, set so it stays on that device and is not copied into backups.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

Sign-in providers

If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, that company's own privacy policy covers what it does when you sign in. We receive only what is described above.

How long we keep it

We keep your records for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and every record in it are deleted immediately and permanently from the live database. The provider's encrypted backups roll over within its retention window, after which no copy remains.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: everything we store is visible and editable in the app.
  • Export: Settings → Export data creates a CSV file of all your entries that you can save or share.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. This cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.

Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU and UK), you may have further rights, such as to request a copy of your data, to object to processing, or to complain to a data protection authority. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined in California law.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change materially affects how your data is used, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
